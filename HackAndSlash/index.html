// CORE FEATURES

GAME_SYSTEMS

Dual-Weapon Combat

Seamlessly swap between a melee blade and a ranged firearm mid-combat. Each weapon feeds the other's resources.

Style & Rank

Dynamic combo system tracks damage variety. Higher style = better rank. Meter decays over time — keep the pressure on.

Weapon Upgrades

Find Upgrade Drones hidden in levels to enhance your arsenal. Each weapon has 3 tiers of upgrades.

Arena Levels

Enter combat arenas where doors lock behind you. Survive waves of enemies to unlock the exit and advance.

Precision Movement

Acceleration-based movement with double jump, crouch, slide, slope handling, and wall detection.

Crosshair Aiming

Free-aim with mouse or right stick. Camera dynamically centers between player and crosshair for tactical awareness.

Dismemberment

Target specific enemy body parts. Limb damage multipliers and physics-driven limb detachment on kill.

Dynamic Audio

AudioMixer with Master/Music/SFX groups. Volume controls persist via PlayerPrefs.

// MOVEMENT & POWERS

PLAYER_ABILITIES

Double Jump

Two jumps allow for aerial agility and platforming. First jump standard, second grants extra height.

Crouch

Reduce hitbox height and move at reduced speed. Essential for low-clearance passages.

Slide

Initiated by crouching while moving at speed. High-velocity, limited-duration evasive maneuver.

Crosshair Aim

Free-aim with mouse/right stick. Camera follows midpoint between player and crosshair (clamped).

Interact

Press Interact near Upgrade Drones to receive weapon upgrades. Context-sensitive action button.

Boost Meter

Passively drains over time. Refilled on enemy hits. When above zero, all melee attacks deal 1.5x damage.

BODY PART & LIMB SYSTEM

Enemies are composed of multiple body parts (Limb, Body, Head). Each part is individually damageable via IDamageable. Severing limbs applies damage multipliers to the main enemy. On death, detached limb prefabs are spawned with physics.

IDamageable interface Limb severance Damage multipliers Physics-based detachment

HEAVY BREAKABLE WALL

Environmental obstacle that shatters when struck by a charging Heavy enemy. Can destroy individual tiles on a Tilemap or the whole object.

ARENA_SYSTEM

COMBAT ARENAS

Trigger zones that lock the player inside when entered. Doors close, enemies spawn in waves, and the exit unlocks only after all hostiles are eliminated.

  • Player entry triggers arena start
  • Doors close (toggle door visibility)
  • Configurable enemies per wave
  • Configurable spawn points
  • Exit opens on wave completion

POST-GAME SUMMARY

After clearing a level, the post-game screen shows animated counters for total kills and clear time. Score-driven feedback loop.

  • Kill counter (animated)
  • Time counter (animated)
  • Triggered at level end

GAME_SYSTEMS_DEEP

STYLE / RANK SYSTEM

Combo & Score Multiplier

Tracks damage events via string keys: Limb, Body, Head, Melee, Range, Dead, Player. Points awarded based on variety of damage types used. Style meter displayed in HUD with a gradient from dark blue to yellow.

  • Damage keys tracked for variety
  • Passive style decay over time
  • Thresholds unlock higher ranks
  • Dark-blue-to-yellow meter gradient

HUD & UI

Real-time Player Feedback

Heads-up display showing four key meters: Style meter, Health meter, Ammo counter, and Power/Boost meter. TextMeshPro-based with reusable panel system.

  • Style meter (combo rank)
  • Health meter (100 HP max)
  • Ammo counter (ranged weapon)
  • Boost/Power meter (melee)

PAUSE & SETTINGS

Menus & Configuration

Full pause menu with resume, settings sliders (Master/Music/SFX volume), and return-to-menu. PlayerPrefs persistence for audio settings.

  • Time scale 0 on pause
  • Volume sliders with real-time preview
  • PlayerPrefs persistence
  • Settings / Upgrades panels

AUDIO ARCHITECTURE

Mixer & ScriptableObjects

Singleton AudioController manages SFX (PlayOneShot) and music (loop) via Unity AudioMixer. Volume routed through exposed parameters.

  • Master / Music / SFX groups
  • AudioData ScriptableObject (clip + name + volume)
  • AudioDataBase SO (music + SFX arrays)
  • Volume control via mixer
